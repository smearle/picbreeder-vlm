Breeding Console

Pick the image(s) you like by clicking them—a selected tile turns green—then press Evolve. Your picks carry over unchanged and the rest of the grid is re-bred from them by mutation and crossover. Press Space or Enter to evolve selected—u or z to undo, r to restart, x to roll again.
