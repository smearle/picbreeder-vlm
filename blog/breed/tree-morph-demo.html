Scrubbable phylogeny demo

A real family tree, but every branch is a CPPN morph you can scrub. Click a tile to expand its children; drag the orange dot on any edge to slide a live morph from the parent image into the child—rendered on the GPU, no stored frames. Drag empty space to pan, scroll to zoom.
